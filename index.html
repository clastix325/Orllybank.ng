What we do

Services built for serious cargo

From a single pallet to a full consignment, we manage the paperwork, logistics and handling so your goods clear every checkpoint without delay.

01 / SEA Freight Forwarding End-to-end coordination of your shipment's journey by sea — booking, documentation, tracking and delivery scheduling, fully managed on your behalf.
02 / IN Import Services We bring your goods into Nigeria efficiently — coordinating with carriers, ports and regulatory bodies to keep your supply chain moving.
03 / OUT Export Services Getting Nigerian goods to global markets, handled with the same rigor — documentation, packaging standards and carrier coordination included.
04 / HVC High Consignment Handling Specialised handling for high-value and high-volume consignments, with extra care on tracking, insurance coordination and secure handover.
05 / CUS Customs Clearance We navigate clearance procedures and paperwork at Nigerian ports, reducing delays and unexpected costs at the border.
